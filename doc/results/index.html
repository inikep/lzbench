Frontier

A result is on the Pareto frontier when no other result is both faster and has a better (lower) ratio. There is one frontier for compression speed (C) and one for decompression speed (D); memcpy is shown as the memory-bandwidth reference and left out of both. Ratio is the compressed size as a percentage of the -byte , so lower is better. Groups are lzbench's own aliases: LZ, SYMMETRIC (BWT, PPM and other codecs with similar compression and decompression speed) and MISC. The data comes from lzbench's doc/results directory; lzbench_json.py there converts lzbench's output for this page.
